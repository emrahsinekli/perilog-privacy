Perilog for Android

Delete your data

Perilog has no accounts. Your health data is stored only on your phone.

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open Perilog.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete All Data and confirm.

Every logged day is erased from your device immediately. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.

Android backup copy

If Android backup is on, a copy may also exist in your own Google account. You can remove it in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup, or at drive.google.com/drive/backups.

Purchase data

If you bought Perilog Pro, our purchase provider RevenueCat keeps an anonymous ID and your purchase history. To delete it, email 1anfibi@gmail.com with the subject “Perilog data deletion”. We will have it deleted within 30 days. Google keeps its own record of the payment under Google's policies.
